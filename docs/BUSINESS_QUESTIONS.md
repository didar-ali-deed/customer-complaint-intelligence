# Business Questions

1. How has published complaint volume changed monthly and annually?
2. Which products, issues, sub-issues, and combinations account for the largest workload?
3. Which issues show material year-over-year growth on a sufficient prior-year base?
4. Which companies receive the highest counts, and how concentrated are complaints?
5. What timely-response rates are observed among complaints with a recorded flag?
6. How do response categories and elapsed days vary by product and company?
7. How do counts vary by state and submission channel, and how have channels changed?
8. How often are narratives available, and how does narrative length vary?
9. Which terms appear in published text? Use consumer narratives where supplied, and clearly distinguish company public-response text when narratives are absent.
10. Which company/product/issue combinations may warrant operational review?

Counts describe published complaints, not incidence or company quality. Compare operational response rates with explicit denominators and consider customer base, market share, publication rules, and geography population before drawing conclusions.
